## Open issues (not fixed)
- **Cost of cultivation is state-level** (not district), as Teammate A's methodology report states.
- **Groundwater values** are from the 2023-24 assessment and are joined to all years as a district descriptor (static). Spot-check a few blocks against the CGWB report.
- **Season windows** in `build_yield_training.py` follow the Indian crop year (July to June): Kharif Jun-Oct, Rabi Nov-Mar of the next year, Summer Apr-Jun of the next year, Whole Year Jul-Jun. Exact dates vary by crop and state, so this is a documented assumption (sources: Arthapedia / DES crop-year definition).
