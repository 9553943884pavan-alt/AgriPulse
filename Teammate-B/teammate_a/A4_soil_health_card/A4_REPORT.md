# A4 — Soil N, P, K & pH (Soil Health Card)

## Methodology & Verification
- **Dataset ID:** A4
- **Publisher:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Soil Health Card Portal (`soilhealth.dac.gov.in`)
- **Coverage:** All 34 agricultural districts in Maharashtra (plus all 757 districts nationwide).
- **Parameters:** `n_kg_per_ha`, `p_kg_per_ha`, `k_kg_per_ha`, `ph`, `organic_carbon_pct`, with official fertility status (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`).
- **Files:**
  - `soil_npk_by_district.csv`: Cleaned Maharashtra district NPK table.
  - `DistrictwiseSoilData_all_india.csv`: All-India master file.
