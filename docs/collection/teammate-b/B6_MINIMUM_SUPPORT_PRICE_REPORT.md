# B6 — Minimum Support Price (`msp.csv`)

## Status: ☑ DONE & VERIFIED

### Methodology & Sources
- **Dataset ID:** B6
- **Publisher:** Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) / Press Information Bureau (PIB), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India.
- **Columns:** `crop_id, crop, season, year, msp_rs_per_quintal, cost_a2_fl_rs_per_quintal, return_over_cost_pct, source_notification`.
- **Coverage:** Official CCEA-approved MSP schedules across `2022-23` through `2025-26` for all mandated Kharif & Rabi crops plus Sugarcane Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP).
