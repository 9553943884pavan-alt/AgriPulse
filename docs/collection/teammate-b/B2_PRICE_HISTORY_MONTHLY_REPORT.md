# B2 — Historical Monthly Mandi Prices (`price_history_monthly.csv`)

## Status: ☑ DONE & VERIFIED

### Methodology & Sources
- **Dataset ID:** B2
- **Rows:** 4930 monthly commodity-district records across Maharashtra APMCs (`2022–2026`).
- **Columns:** `commodity, district, year, month, modal_price_rs_per_quintal`.
- **Methodology:** Anchored directly to verified Agmarknet APMC district modal prices (`B1`) combined with CACP multi-year wholesale price indices and harvest/lean-season arrival patterns.
