# B4 — Itemized Cost of Cultivation for Maharashtra (`cost_of_cultivation.csv`)

## Status: ☑ DONE & VERIFIED

### Methodology & Sources
- **Dataset ID:** B4
- **Publisher:** Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / CACP *Comprehensive Scheme for Studying the Cost of Cultivation of Principal Crops in India* (Maharashtra State Tables) + MPKV Rahuri Department of Agricultural Economics bulletins for horticulture crops.
- **Crops Covered:** 16 major Maharashtra crops (far exceeding the `>= 6 crops` threshold in Step 0 Check #9).
- **Exact Itemized Columns (Rs/ha):**
  - `seed_rs_per_ha`
  - `fertilizer_rs_per_ha`
  - `manure_rs_per_ha`
  - `human_labour_rs_per_ha`
  - `bullock_labour_rs_per_ha`
  - `machine_labour_rs_per_ha`
  - `irrigation_rs_per_ha`
  - `pesticide_rs_per_ha`
  - `interest_rs_per_ha`
  - `cost_a2_fl_rs_per_ha` (Paid-out expenses + imputed family labour)
  - `cost_c2_rs_per_ha` (Comprehensive cost including imputed land rent & fixed capital interest)
